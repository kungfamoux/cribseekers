# CribSeekers Frontend Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0.0 (Release Candidate 1)

## Overview

This roadmap outlines the planned development and enhancement of the CribSeekers frontend application. The roadmap is organized by phases and includes short-term, medium-term, and long-term goals.
